# Privacy Policy: Word Filter for X

_Last updated: October 2026_

Word Filter for X does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the extension does with your data

- **Post text on x.com and twitter.com:** the extension reads the text of posts on your screen only to decide whether to hide them. That text is processed on your device and never leaves it.
- **Your settings and word list:** these are saved with your browser's built-in extension storage. If you use browser sync (for example Chrome Sync or Firefox Sync), your browser may sync them across your own devices. The developer cannot see them.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
- It does not send data to the developer or to any third party.
- It does not make network requests.

## The Support page

The Support page, opened from the **Support this project** button, is a local page inside the extension. It contacts nobody on its own. Links on it (for example Buy Me a Coffee, or the project links) open in a new tab only when you click them, and are then governed by those sites' own policies.

## Permissions

- **storage:** saves your settings and word list.
- **Access to x.com and twitter.com:** lets the extension hide matching posts on those sites. It has no access to any other website.

## Open source

The full source code is available at <https://github.com/pechen987/X-post-filter> so you can verify these statements.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/pechen987/X-post-filter/issues>.
